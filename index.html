Each point is a reel. X = skip rate %, Y = reach. Lower skip rate means more viewers watched — but reach is also driven by initial distribution. A low-skip, high-reach post is the ideal combination.
Saves/1k vs. New Followers · All Posts
Saves per 1k reach is the strongest predictor of follower conversion in the current dataset. High saves signal content worth keeping — and the algorithm rewards that with more distribution.
Sends/1k vs. New Followers · All Posts
Sends drive distribution to new audiences — they push reach up but don't directly convert viewers to followers. High sends with low followers = viral potential not being captured.
Comments/1k vs. New Followers · All Posts
Comments are a weak follower conversion signal. They signal existing community engagement more than new audience attraction. Don't optimise for comments at the expense of reach.
